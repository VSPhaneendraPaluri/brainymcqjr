Getting Started Guide 📖

1. How to Register?

  1. Click on the "Register" button on the home page
  2. Enter a username (e.g., "john_doe")
  3. Enter your email address
  4. Create a password (minimum 6 characters)
  5. Confirm your password
  6. Click "Register" button
  7. You will be redirected to the login page

2. How to Login?

  1. Click on the "Login" button
  2. Enter your username
  3. Enter your password
  4. Click the "Login" button
  5. You will be directed to your personal dashboard

3. How to Take a Quiz?

  1. From your Dashboard, click "Start New Quiz"
  2. Review the quiz guidelines
  3. Click "Begin Quiz" to start
  4. You will have 60 minutes to complete the quiz
  5. The quiz contains 50 questions:
    • 25 Mathematics questions
    • 25 Science questions
  6. Select one option (A, B, C, or D) for each question
  7. Use "Previous" and "Next" buttons to navigate
  8. Review the Questions Overview on the side to track progress

4. How to Submit Quiz?

  1. After answering all questions, click "Submit Quiz" button
  2. Your answers will be automatically evaluated
  3. You will see your results with:
    • Mathematics score (out of 25)
    • Science score (out of 25)
    • Total score (out of 50)
    • Time taken
    • Percentage scored

5. How to View Score History?

  1. Click on "Dashboard" from the navigation menu
  2. You will see your "Quiz History" table
  3. The table shows all your previous quiz attempts with:
    • Date and time of the quiz
    • Mathematics score
    • Science score
    • Total score
    • Time spent

6. Important Tips

  • ✓ Always keep your password safe and secure
  • ✓ Don't share your login credentials with others
  • ✓ Questions are randomly selected for each quiz
  • ✓ You can take as many quizzes as you want
  • ✓ Your score history is maintained for each attempt
  • ✓ Review your results to identify areas of improvement
  • ✓ Practice regularly to improve your scores

7. System Requirements

  • ✓ Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • ✓ Internet connection for first access
  • ✓ JavaScript enabled in your browser
  • ✓ Cookies enabled for session management

8. Troubleshooting

Forgot Password?
Contact your administrator or create a new account with a different username.
Session Timeout?
Your session may expire after a long period of inactivity. Login again to continue.
Quiz Not Loading?
Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. Ensure JavaScript is enabled.
Can't See My Scores?
Make sure you have submitted the quiz completely. Check your Dashboard for all attempts.

For more help, please contact the administrator or visit the Help section again.
